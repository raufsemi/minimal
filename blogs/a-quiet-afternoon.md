title: A Quiet Afternoon
date: 2026-10-07
tags: [demo, writing]
---

Sometimes an afternoon feels longer than it actually is.

The room is quiet. The fan turns slowly above you. Outside, someone closes a gate and a motorcycle disappears down the road.

Nothing important happened.

Maybe that is the point.

We spend so much time looking for moments worth remembering that we forget how ordinary most memories actually are.

A cup of coffee.
A message left unanswered.
Rain against a window.
Someone laughing in another room.

None of these things ask to be understood.

They simply happen.

And then, eventually, they become yesterday.

Perhaps a life is mostly made of things like this.

Small moments passing through us before we realize they were moments at all.
